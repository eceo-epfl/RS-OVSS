1 An agricultural area is a wide open space used to grow crops or to hold farm animals, usually enclosed by a fence, hedge or other barrier
2 A building is a closed structure with walls and a roof with residential, religious  or industrial space
3 A bush forest is an area covered with shrubs or plants branching out from the base
4 A forest is a relatively dense wooded area consisting of one or more stands of trees
5 A glacier is a surface covered mainly by ice or eternal snow
6 A lake is a stagnant body of water that is immobile permanent or periodic such as reservoirs ponds pools 
7 A wetland is an area of marsh, swamp, bog or wet meadow covered with vegetation with permanent, temporary, stagnant or flowing water
8 A vineyard is a grape plantation, especially one used in the production of wine 
9 The railways  include  standard-gauge, narrow-gauge, and small-gauge railroads for multi-track sections and in station areas
10 A river is a watercourse or a stream where water flows for most of the year
11 The road generally includes  well-developed  roads that are   passable by all vehicles   traffic area  public parking spaces and private parking spaces
12 The rocks area includes cliffs solid rock   boulders  stones scree and rubble found either in gravel pits or on mountain slopes 
13 An open forest is a sparsely wooded area with vegetation on the ground and grassland
14 A scattered rocks area is an area of loose rock, screes, boulders and rockfill partially covered with grass
